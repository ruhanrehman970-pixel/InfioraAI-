FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will Nexora just do my homework for me?

No. Nexora is built to help you understand. Tutors can switch to Socratic mode, and the Writing Assistant explains every change it suggests so you learn as you go.

Which languages are supported?

Translation covers 107 languages, including Urdu, Arabic, Hindi, Bengali, Persian, Chinese, Spanish and French. Right-to-left languages display correctly throughout the app.

Can teachers use Nexora with their class?

Yes. Choose "Teacher" when you sign up to unlock lesson plans, worksheets, rubrics, grading feedback and auto-generated class quizzes.

What happens to the files I upload?

Your files stay private to your account. You can download or delete everything at any time from your settings.

Can I cancel Pro anytime?

Yes. Your account drops back to the Free plan at the end of the billing period, and nothing is lost.
